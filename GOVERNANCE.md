# Governance

LudoWeave Engine is currently maintained through a lightweight, role-based model suitable for an early open-source project.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose issues, documentation, tests, code, or design feedback under the DCO.
- **Reviewers** provide trusted review in an area such as architecture, quality, security, packaging, or rendering.
- **Maintainers** triage work, merge changes, manage releases, enforce community policy, and protect project scope.
- **Founding maintainer** resolves decisions when consensus cannot be reached while the maintainer group is still small.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive participation. Maintainers may add or remove roles transparently based on project needs, inactivity, conduct, or security concerns.

## Decisions

Routine changes use issue and pull-request review. Maintainers seek consensus and prefer evidence from tests, benchmarks, platform results, and user needs.

Accepted Architecture Decision Records govern local architectural choices. A public RFC is required before changing compatibility policy, persistent schemas, native languages, renderer or platform backends, licensing, governance, security boundaries, networking, editor scope, or 3D scope.

Stable public APIs and persistent schemas should receive two maintainer approvals once the maintainer pool supports that rule. During pre-alpha, no symbol is implicitly stable.

## Releases and conflicts

Only maintainers may publish official releases. A reviewer must recuse themselves from decisions where they have a material conflict of interest. Security and conduct reports follow their dedicated private policies.
